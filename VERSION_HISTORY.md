# Gabriel the Brave Website - Version History

## Version 4.1.0 (Current)
**Release Date:** November 20, 2025
**Status:** Ready for Deployment

### 🚀 New Features
*   **Audiobook Preview:** Added a "Listen for Free" section to `product.html` with a secure, non-downloadable MP3 player.
*   **Curriculum Builder:** Launched a fully interactive lesson planner in `curriculum_builder.html` with "Marketplace" style selection and Print/PDF generation.
*   **Press Kit:** Added a dedicated Press Kit section to `news.html` with high-resolution downloadable 3D book renders.
*   **Activities Gallery:** Revamped `activities.html` into a masonry-style gallery focused exclusively on coloring pages.

### 🎨 Design & Content Updates
*   **Retailer Integration:** Added official logos for Walmart, Barnes & Noble, and Amazon to `news.html` and `product.html`.
*   **Resources Hub:** Updated `resources.html` with distinct, graphical cards linking to the new tools.
*   **News Feed:** Added the latest "KidlioMag" review to the press page.
*   **Standardization:** Applied V4 Header and Footer templates across all pages for consistent navigation.

### 🔧 Technical Improvements
*   **Asset Organization:** Created dedicated folders for `assets/audio`, `assets/images/logos`, and `assets/images/coloring`.
*   **Performance:** Replaced heavy WAV audio with optimized MP3 format.
*   **Link Validation:** Verified all internal and external links (0 broken links found).
