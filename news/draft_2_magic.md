"Like Unwrapping a Warm Blanket": Marine Vet and Tech Exec Create New Christmas Classic

FORT WORTH, Texas – November 19, 2025 – As families prepare for the holidays, a new book born from a father's love is rekindling the magic of Christmas wonder. Gabriel the Brave: A Christmas Story has officially launched, offering a heartwarming answer to the age-old question: "Why do we put an angel at the top of the tree?"

The book is a collaboration between Author Carter Amon and Illustrator Darryl Erby. It is deeply personal, featuring touching dedications to their families. Amon writes to his sons: "You are my armor every time I leave for work. You are my smile when I am nervous. You are my shining light when I am lost."

Producer Darryl Erby adds, "Carter and I loved spending Christmas together as kids. Now as fathers, we want to share why we celebrate and share that love with others."

Critics are calling it a modern classic. Kidliomag praised the book, stating:

"It’s like unwrapping a warm, cozy blanket on a chilly winter morning – you feel instantly comforted and inspired... It’s a must-have for every family’s holiday collection."

About the Illustrations
Illustrator Darryl Erby, a tech executive with a passion for data analytics, utilized a unique AI-driven approach to create consistent and vibrant design elements, proving that technology can help tell the most timeless of human stories.
