From Second Grade Best Friends to Co-Authors: Marine Veteran and Tech Executive Launch Heartwarming Christmas Book, Gabriel the Brave

DALLAS-FORT WORTH, TX – [Insert Date]

This question, asked by author Carter Amon’s sons one Christmas, sparked a passion project that would eventually bring two lifelong friends together to create a new holiday classic. Gabriel the Brave is Carter’s first authored book, born of his wife Sarah's inspiration and the curiosity of their children. It tells the enchanting story of a shy angel named Gabriel who finds his courage to deliver the message of Jesus' birth and light the Bethlehem star.

The project is a labor of love rooted in a bond that began over three decades ago. Carter Amon and illustrator Darryl Erby first met in the second grade and have been friends for over 33 years. Their shared history and values are woven into the fabric of the story, which is dedicated to their own families.

In a touching dedication to his sons, Amon writes, “You are my armor every time I leave for work. You are my smile when I am nervous. You are my shining light when I am lost.” He also credits his wife, Sarah, noting that the story "would never have happened if it wasn’t for your idea for the book and constant support."

The book combines traditional storytelling with modern innovation. Illustrator Darryl Erby utilized a cutting-edge AI-driven approach to create the book's consistent and vibrant visual elements, bringing Gabriel's journey to life in a way that captivates young readers.

About the Author and Illustrator

Carter Amon (Author) is a Marine Corps veteran with a bachelor's degree in business. He resides in the Dallas-Fort Worth Metroplex in Texas with his wife, Sarah, a Nurse Practitioner, and their two boys. Gabriel the Brave is Carter’s first authored book and a passion project born of Sarah's inspiration from a simple question their sons asked one year: "Why do we put an angel at the top of the tree?"

Darryl Erby (Illustrator & Designer) is a software sales executive with an MBA in Data Analytics and a deep-seated passion for technology. He and his wife, Jessica, live in the Dallas-Fort Worth area with their two sons. Darryl utilized an AI-driven approach to create consistent and vibrant illustrations and design elements in Gabriel the Brave.

Gabriel the Brave: A Christmas Story is available now on Amazon.
