**"Gabriel the Brave" Earns 5-Star Seal from Readers' Favorite**

**DALLAS-FORT WORTH, TX, December 3, 2025** – The creators of "Gabriel the Brave: A Christmas Story" are proud to announce that the book has received a 5-Star rating from Readers' Favorite, one of the largest and most respected book review and award contest sites on the internet.

This prestigious 5-Star seal recognizes books for their high-quality writing, illustration, and overall reading experience. "Gabriel the Brave" received top marks in multiple categories, including Appearance, Plot, and Development, culminating in a 5-Star overall opinion.

The official review from Readers' Favorite praises the book as a "delightful Christmas story," with reviewer Pikasho Deka calling it "a captivating Christmas story that illustrates the importance of courage, family, and faith."

The review highlights Darryl Erby's "mesmerizing" illustrations and Carter Amon's "adventurous narrative that young readers are bound to love." It commends the book for its handling of relatable themes, noting that Gabriel "experiences fear and anxiety when tasked with an important responsibility, something youngsters can relate to. Through his faith in God, he overcomes self-doubt and accomplishes everything he sets out to do, which is a great life lesson for kids."

"We are incredibly honored and humbled to receive this 5-Star seal," said co-creator Darryl Erby. "Our goal was always to create a story that was not only beautiful to look at but also deeply meaningful. This award is a wonderful validation of the heart and soul we poured into 'Gabriel the Brave.'"

The full review can be found on the Readers' Favorite website. "Gabriel the Brave" is available for purchase on Amazon.

**About Gabriel the Brave:**
"Gabriel the Brave" is a children's book that tells the story of a shy young angel chosen to announce the birth of Jesus Christ. It is a story of courage, faith, and the true meaning of Christmas, designed to bring families together.

**About Tymbr Books:**
Tymbr Books is a publisher focused on creating beautiful, meaningful stories that inspire faith and courage in young readers.
