"Gabriel the Brave" Launches Free Companion Resources for Families and Sunday Schools

DALLAS-FORT WORTH, TX, December 3, 2025

To further its mission of sharing faith and courage with families, the creators of the 5-Star award-winning book "Gabriel the Brave" have launched a new collection of free digital resources, available now on its official website.

The new resources are designed to complement the book and provide families and educators with engaging, faith-based activities. The free collection includes:

  • A full-length Audiobook: Experience the story of "Gabriel the Brave" in a new, immersive audio format, perfect for bedtime or travel.
  • Printable Bible Study and Coloring Sheets: Deepen the connection to the story’s themes with guided lessons and creative coloring pages that bring key moments to life.

These materials are designed for parents, guardians, and Sunday school teachers to use as tools to discuss the book’s central messages of faith, courage, and the true meaning of Christmas.

"We want the story of Gabriel to be as accessible as possible," said co-creator Carter Amon. "By providing the audiobook, coloring sheets, and a bible study for free, we hope to give families and churches new ways to connect with the story and with each other."

All new resources can be accessed now on the official "Gabriel the Brave" website at www.gabrielthebrave.com/resources.html.

About Gabriel the Brave:

"Gabriel the Brave" is a 5-Star award-winning children's book that tells the story of a shy young angel chosen to announce the birth of Jesus Christ. It is a story of courage, faith, and the true meaning of Christmas, designed to bring families together.
